About Saebom Church

새봄교회 (Saebom Church) is a Protestant church in Seongnam, Gyeonggi-do, Republic of Korea. It belongs to The General Assembly of Presbyterian Church in Korea (HapDong), Seongnam Presbytery — in Korean, 대한예수교장로회(합동) 성남노회. Senior Pastor: Rev. Shin Cheol. The church is registered with the Korean National Tax Service as a non-profit corporation (비영리법인), registration number 129-82-63641.

What we do

  • Sunday worship at 11:00, Sunday afternoon worship at 13:30, Wednesday worship at 19:30, and daily early-morning prayer at 5:00.
  • A school for the next generation on Sundays at 13:30: children, youth, and young adults.
  • Saebom Church supports missionaries serving in several countries in Asia and North Africa, as well as a military base church and a partner church (Saehim Church) in Korea, through prayer and giving.
  • Each week the church bulletin carries one section of the Westminster Confession of Faith, read together.

This website

https://saebomchurch.org is the official website of 새봄교회, built and operated by the church. It collects no personal data: there are no forms, no accounts, no cookies, and no analytics.
